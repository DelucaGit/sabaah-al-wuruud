# Adhkar text: sources and licence

The files `src/data/adhkar/morning.json`, `evening.json`, `sleep.json` and `istikhara.json` (plus the hand-entered `home.json` and `travel.json`, see below) hold
text from **Hisn al-Muslim** (حصن المسلم من أذكار الكتاب والسنة) by Dr. Sa'id ibn Ali ibn Wahf
al-Qahtani (سعيد بن علي بن وهف القحطاني), in Arabic only. They are generated by
`scripts/build-adhkar-data.mjs`; do not edit them by hand, change the script and re-run it:

```sh
node scripts/build-adhkar-data.mjs            # downloads the sources into the OS temp dir
node scripts/build-adhkar-data.mjs ./cache    # or into a cache directory of your choice
```

| C | [rn0x/hisn_almuslim_json](https://github.com/rn0x/hisn_almuslim_json) | Manual tie-break only (sleep: «اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ» 3×). Not used by the script. |

How they are combined:

- The Arabic text always comes from **A**. Instructions and count notes around the dhikr
  (`(( ))`, `(ثلاثَ مرَّاتٍ)`, `[وإذا أمسى قال: …]`) are removed, because `repeat` carries the count.
- Morning and evening are one chapter in A. The book says which adhkar are only for the morning
  («إذا أصبح») or only for the evening («إذا أمسى»), and gives the evening wording in a note
  (without tashkeel). The script produces the evening wording by explicit word substitutions on
  A's morning text and **fails unless the result matches B's evening text letter for letter**
  (tashkeel ignored).
- Every other text is compared with B; the wording (letters) is identical for all non-Quran items.
  Remaining differences in tashkeel or count are listed below and in the script's report.

Smaller notes: the opening line «الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَحْدَهُ، وَالصَّلاَةُ وَالسَّلاَمُ عَلَى مَنْ لاَ نَبِيَّ بَعْدَهُ»
that B lists as the first morning/evening item is the chapter's opening, not a dhikr with a count, and
is left out (A leaves it out too). Footnotes in B that describe *when/how* a dhikr is said rather than
its virtue (sleep-04, sleep-06) are not stored as `virtue`; three tangled takhreej footnotes (sleep-04,
sleep-06, sleep-13) are written out explicitly in the script from B's own text. The book has both
«وَشَرَكِهِ» (morning/evening) and «وَشِرْكِهِ» (sleep) in the same du'a; both are kept as given.

## Home and travel (hand-entered)

`home.json` and `travel.json` are **not** generated by the script. They were entered by hand from
Hisn al-Muslim's chapters دعاء الخروج من المنزل, دعاء دخول المنزل and دعاء الركوب / دعاء السفر,
with the book's takhreej in `source`. `home.json` is one dataset with two parts, told apart by the
optional `heading` field (leaving the house: home-01, home-02; entering: home-03). `travel.json`:
the takbir and Az-Zukhruf 43:13-14 (travel-01), the du'a on setting out (travel-02, Muslim 2/978)
and the words added on returning (travel-03). They are not yet compared against sources A and B.
**Every word and its tashkeel needs human review (Aisha) before release** (issue #90).

## Istikhara

`istikhara.json` is the du'a only, from «اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ» to «ثُمَّ أَرْضِنِي بِهِ»
(Jabir's hadith introduction and the closing remark are cut). The narrator's remarks inside the du'a
(«وَيُسَمِّي حَاجَتَهُ», «أَوْ قَالَ: عَاجِلِهِ وَآجِلِهِ») are kept as the book prints them.
`highlight` is «هَذَا الْأَمْرَ», which occurs twice (the matter if it is good, the matter if it is bad);
the UI highlights every occurrence. Source: البخاري، برقم 1162.
